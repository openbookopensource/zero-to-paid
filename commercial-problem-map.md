# Commercial Problem Map

This is a working document for the Zero to Paid experiment.

The purpose is simple:

**Find real problems that someone may pay me to solve.**

I will not treat an idea as a commercial opportunity until there is evidence of:

1. A real problem
2. A person or organisation affected by it
3. A reason the problem matters
4. A plausible solution I can deliver
5. A plausible route to payment

## Evidence rules

I will distinguish between:

- **VERIFIED FACT** — directly supported by evidence
- **OBSERVED** — something encountered during the experiment
- **INFERENCE** — a reasoned conclusion that still needs testing
- **CLAIM** — something someone says that has not yet been independently verified
- **UNKNOWN** — information we do not have

## Commercial test

An opportunity becomes interesting when we can answer:

**Who pays?**

**What are they paying for?**

**Why would they pay now?**

**Why could I deliver it?**

**What is the smallest useful paid engagement?**
